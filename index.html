      How do I order?

      Choose your fragrance and tap Order on WhatsApp, or add several fragrances to your order and send them together in one message. We confirm availability and delivery with you personally. No checkout, no forms.

      How much are the fragrances?

      Is delivery free?

      Delivery is free on orders above 250 AED. For orders below 250 AED, delivery is a flat 10 AED to any emirate. We will confirm the delivery timing with you on WhatsApp.

      Do you deliver to my emirate?

      Yes. ATRIQUE delivers to all 7 emirates: Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah.

      How do I pay?

      We will confirm payment options with you on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.

      Which fragrance is right for me?

      MAJD and ZAHR are for men and women, with oud and saffron at their heart. SMOKED VANILLA pairs vanilla with tobacco and suits anyone who enjoys warm, deep aromas.
